# PRMEhomelab

My personal homelab project focused on building practical experience in Linux system administration, networking, troubleshooting, security, and infrastructure management.

The lab is designed to simulate some of the responsibilities of a real-world systems administrator while giving me a place to experiment, make mistakes, troubleshoot problems, and apply what I learn.

## Objectives

* Build confidence administering and managing Linux servers.
* Gain hands-on experience with RHEL and Linux administration.
* Develop practical networking and troubleshooting skills.
* Learn to diagnose and resolve problems independently.
* Prepare for the Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) exam.
* Practice good habits around security, maintenance, backups, and documentation.
* Experiment with self-hosted services and infrastructure.

## Self-Hosted Services

The lab may be used to experiment with services such as:

* Immich
* Pi-hole
* Web services
* Git services
* Other services as the lab develops

## Why I'm Learning

The main goal is to turn the theory I learn into practical experience. Rather than only studying Linux and infrastructure, I want to understand what it is actually like to operate and maintain systems.

This homelab also gives me a place to build experience that is relevant to working as a Linux system administrator and eventually moving into systems engineering.

## Documentation

Documentation covers more than just the final configuration. I also document troubleshooting, problems encountered, design decisions, and lessons learned so the repository reflects the experience of actually building and maintaining the environment.
